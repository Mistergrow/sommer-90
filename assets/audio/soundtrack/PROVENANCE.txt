SOMMER '90 — ORIGINAL TRACKER SOUNDTRACK
Created for this project on 2026-09-05 with AI coding assistance.

The 56 MOD files were generated from newly specified scores. Generation 1
(twelve tracks, 2026-09-05) keeps its score in tools/build_original_soundtrack.py
and is individually arranged by tools/soundtrack_legacy.py (revision 2026-09-24).
Generation 2 (24 tracks, 2026-09-24) is written as chord progressions and
melody bars in tools/soundtrack_songs.py, arranged into ProTracker patterns by
tools/soundtrack_arranger.py. Generation 3 (20 tracks, 2026-09-24) consists of
fully arranged 4/6/8-channel productions scored in tools/soundtrack_songs_v3.py
and produced by tools/soundtrack_studio.py (separate drum voices, builds, drops,
key changes, counter voices, fades). All embedded instrument PCM samples are
computed by tools/soundtrack_synth.py
using oscillators, Karplus-Strong strings, FM, filters, noise, a small
Schroeder reverb and envelopes.
No third-party recordings, sample banks, module patterns or melody sequences
are inputs to the generator. The original MOD archive is never read by it.

These are project-created assets, not licensed recordings of the historical
musicians named in the game's collectible catalog. No third-party music
license is relied on for these generated assets. This provenance record does
not claim a human composer, exclusive copyright, or legal certification of
musical uniqueness. The project's owner controls distribution of the project.

Reproduce: python tools/build_original_soundtrack.py
Verify:   python tools/build_original_soundtrack.py --check
Manifest: data/soundtrack.json (file SHA-256, titles, tempos, durations)

Craft calibration (generation 3, 2026-09-24): aggregate technique statistics
(effect frequencies such as portamento/vibrato, interval profiles, repetition,
timbre changes per channel) were measured on a local, never-distributed archive
of 1990s tracker modules to decide WHICH techniques the arranger should use.
No notes, patterns, orders or samples of those modules were read into, copied
or reproduced by any generator; the analysis script only emits counts.

Slot mapping: tools/assign_soundtrack_slots.py assigns each historical slot a
soundtrack MOD whose era fits the slot year (python tools/assign_soundtrack_slots.py --check).

The old catalog keeps historical slot titles/years for story progression and
matching player-provided MODs. Those recordings are not part of the soundtrack
or the exported game. The in-game now-playing display credits the actual file.

Personal MOD files: Options > MOD folder & soundtrack. Add lawfully usable
ProTracker .mod files (4/6/8 channels), then Rescan. Unmatched files are available
as personal stereo tracks. Missing/invalid slot overrides use the soundtrack.

Production revision (2026-09-24): all 56 files regenerated reproducibly.
The twelve founding scores retain themes, keys, tempos and 64-bar duration,
with individual instrument palettes. Generation 1/2 gains phrase dynamics,
explicit releases and whole-mix endings. Synthesis corrects plucked-string
feedback tuning and adds attack ramps before unchanged sustain loops.
The studio arranger corrects shared-channel handoffs, percussion collisions,
volume articulation and final fades. No reference score/sample is imported.
See docs/SOUNDTRACK_REVIEW_2026-09-24.md for comparison, measured validation
and the limits of a technical (rather than subjective listening) assessment.

Musical revision (second pass, 2026-09-24): shaped oscillator attacks and
spectral decay, revised original drum synthesis, newly written call/response
phrases and individual closing responses, voice-led bass registers and
whole-phrase octave placement. Studio counter voices leave passing notes
un-doubled; automatic ornaments are confined to phrase endings.
The six historical modules were analyzed for general musical techniques and
sample envelopes. No historical sample, pattern or melody is a generator input.
Local reference listening excerpts live only under out/, outside shipped assets.
See docs/SOUNDTRACK_MUSICAL_REVISION_2026-09-24.md and the local level-matched
review at out/soundtrack-musical-review-2026-09-24/index.html.

Intro revision (2026-09-25): original opening signatures, instrument dialogues,
harmonically written middle episodes and motif reprises. The title cue now
introduces its identity at the start of the visible cracktro. Its main theme
enters after four bars rather than eight. Tempos, lengths and channel counts
remain unchanged. Existing synthesized instruments remain the sound basis.
Nine historical modules were inspected for general opening/form techniques;

See docs/INTRO_REFERENCE_ANALYSIS_2026-09-25.md and

Techno/rave revision (2026-09-25, following the intro revision): selected dance
scores gain original multi-bar bass conversations, syncopated piano/rave chord
stabs, authored drum variations and contrasting breaks/builds/returns. The
title theme gains a piano-house groove while retaining its immediate motif.
New oscillator-generated resonant acid samples have closed/open/accent colors
and sustained loops for classic tracker portamento. No commercial recording,
sample pack, historical riff or drum-break recording is used as generator input.
See docs/SOUNDTRACK_RAVE_REVISION_2026-09-25.md and
